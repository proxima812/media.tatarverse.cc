name: "National Corpus of the Crimean Tatar Language"
description: "Online corpus of annotated Crimean Tatar texts, created as part of Ukraine's state strategy for language development."
facts:
  - "Initiated by Ukraine's Ministry of Reintegration, implemented by the NGO QIRIM Young together with the Eastern Europe Foundation/EGAP and Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv"
  - "Built on the Sketch Engine platform"
  - "Includes a transliteration tool"
  - "Interface available in English, Ukrainian, and Crimean Tatar"
