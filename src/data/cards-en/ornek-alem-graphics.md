---
name: "Örnek"
description: "A project by the Ukrainian NGO ALEM dedicated to preserving and promoting the traditional Crimean Tatar örnek ornament."
facts:
  - "In 2021, örnek and the knowledge associated with it were inscribed on the UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage list"
  - "The ornament is used in embroidery, weaving, ceramics, wood carving, jewelry, and glass painting"
  - "The project's English-language site tells the history and symbolism of örnek for an international audience"
