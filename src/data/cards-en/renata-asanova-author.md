---
name: "Renata Asanova"
description: "A Crimean Tatar-Ukrainian artist whose paintings explore the identity of deported Crimean Tatars and the traditional \"ornek\" ornament."
facts:
  - "Born in 1992 to a family of deported Crimean Tatars in Uzbekistan, lives in Kyiv"
  - "Works in oil, pastel, acrylic, and graphic art"
  - "The \"Crimea and Home\" series explores inherited identity and roots"
  - "Participant in the Artists at Risk program, has exhibited in Berlin and Lithuania"
