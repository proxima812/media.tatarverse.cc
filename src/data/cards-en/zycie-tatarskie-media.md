---
name: "Życie Tatarskie"
description: "A Polish-language magazine of Poland's Tatar minority about the life, history, and Muslim culture of Polish Tatars."
facts:
  - "Successor to a prewar Vilnius publication from 1934-1939, revived in 1998"
  - "Published by the Cultural and Educational Union of Tatars of Poland in Białystok"
  - "Issued bimonthly"
  - "Covers the historic Tatar villages of Bohoniki and Kruszyniany"
