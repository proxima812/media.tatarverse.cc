media.tatarverse is an open, non-commercial catalog of links to other people's
projects about the Tatar, Bashkir and Crimean Tatar languages and cultures.
There is no sign-up, no accounts, and nothing is sold. We collect only what the
site needs to work.

## What is stored in your browser

The site is static and has no backend, so two things are kept locally and never
sent anywhere:

- **Saved projects** ("Saved") - a list of card ids in `localStorage`.
- **Catalog view** - grid or list.

These are not cookies: the data does not travel with any request. Clearing site
data in your browser erases both, and the saved list along with them.

## Analytics

On the current build, web analytics is **disabled**: neither Google Tag Manager
nor Yandex Metrica is connected, their scripts are not loaded and they set no
cookies. If analytics is ever enabled, this page will say so, and such cookies
will be governed by that service's own policy (Google, Yandex) - you can block
them in your browser settings.

Like any web server, the hosting provider may record technical request data: IP
address, device and browser type, the page requested. Those records exist for
diagnosing failures, are not tied to a name or an email, and are not used for
advertising.

## The "Add a project" form

A submission from the form on the "[Add a project](/en/add)" page travels like
this: the form (the [Tally](https://tally.so) service) - our handler - a
**public issue in the
[GitHub repository](https://github.com/proxima812/media.tatarverse.cc)**, from
which a card is then written.

Hence the main point: **everything you type into the form becomes public** - the
text of the submission is visible to anyone who opens the repository, and it
stays in the history. Do not put personal details you would rather not publish
into the form. A project name, a link and a contact you are comfortable showing
are enough.

Tally's own privacy policy applies to the form, and GitHub's terms apply to the
repository.

If you would rather not use the form, send a pull request or write to us any of
the ways listed under "Contact" below.

## Data in the catalog cards

A card describes a public project and contains only what the project has already
published: name, description, link, logo. We do not collect or publish authors'
contact details.

All cards live in an open repository, so their edit history is public.

## Links to third-party sites

The catalog is essentially a list of links. Following one takes you off
media.tatarverse, and from there the policy of that site applies. We do not
control how third-party projects handle your data.

## Your rights

If your personal data has ended up somewhere on the site - in a project
description, for example - write to us any of the ways below and we will remove
it. Requests from project representatives are handled first.

## Children

The site does not deliberately collect data and is not aimed at children under
13.

## Changes to this policy

This text may be updated, for example when new functionality appears. The date
of the last change is shown at the top of the page, and the full edit history is
open in the repository.
