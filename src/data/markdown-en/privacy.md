media.tatarverse is an open, non-commercial catalog: it links to other
people's projects about the Tatar, Bashkir, and Crimean Tatar languages and
cultures. We don't require an account, don't sell access, and don't collect
more data than the site needs to work.

## What we collect

There's no submission form to fill in - new cards enter the catalog through a
pull request on GitHub, and GitHub's own terms apply to that process, not ours.

While browsing, the hosting provider and, on builds where they're enabled,
web-analytics services (for example Google Tag Manager or Yandex Metrica) may
automatically log technical information: IP address, device and browser type,
pages visited, links clicked in the catalog. None of this is tied to a name or
email - we never ask for either.

## How we use it

Only to understand which parts of the catalog are useful and to fix what's
broken. We don't sell or share data with third parties for advertising - the
site has no ad network of its own.

## Cookies

Technical cookies (for example, remembering whether you last viewed the
catalog as a grid or a list) are stored locally in your browser and never sent
to a server. If a build has analytics enabled, its cookies follow that
service's own policy (Google, Yandex) - you can disable them in your browser
settings at any time.

## Links to other sites

The catalog is, essentially, a list of links. Following one takes you off
media.tatarverse, and the privacy policy of that site applies from then on. We
don't review or control how listed projects handle your data.

## Storage and security

Catalog cards and their logos live in a public GitHub repository - anyone can
inspect the change history. Hosting logs are kept only as long as needed to
diagnose issues and are deleted automatically.

## Your rights

If you believe your personal data appears anywhere on the site without
consent (for example, inside a project description), reach out - see
"[Contact](#contact)" below - and we'll remove it.

## Children

The site does not knowingly collect data from anyone and is not directed at
children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this text, for example when adding new site functionality. The
date at the top of the page reflects the last change; substantial changes are
also visible in the repository's commit history.

## Contact

Questions about privacy - open an issue in the
[project's GitHub repository](https://github.com/proxima812/media.tatarverse.cc).
